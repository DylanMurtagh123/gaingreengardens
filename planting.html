— Signs your borders need attention

When beds and borders
need refreshing.

Planting areas can quickly lose their shape when weeds, tired plants and bare patches take over. A focused border refresh can make the garden feel more cared for without needing a full redesign.

Bare or patchy borders

Empty gaps, thin planting and exposed soil can make beds look unfinished. New planting and mulch can create a fuller, cleaner appearance.

Weeds taking over

When weeds start competing with plants, borders can quickly look messy. Clearing and preparing the bed gives new planting a better start.

Overgrown planting

Plants that have spread too far can hide paths, edges and smaller shrubs. Tidying and reshaping helps restore balance to the garden.

— Our planting process

How we refresh
beds and borders.

A good planting job starts before the new plants go in. We look at the space, clear unwanted growth where needed, prepare the bed and consider practical details such as light, access, existing plants and how much ongoing care you want.

This helps create planting areas that look neater, feel more balanced and are easier to maintain after the visit. The aim is a tidy, natural finish that suits the garden and stays practical to look after.

Our approach:

  • Assess the bed, border or planting area
  • Clear weeds, tired growth or unwanted plants where needed
  • Prepare the soil surface before planting
  • Add practical planting for colour, structure or coverage
  • Finish with mulch where suitable for a cleaner look
