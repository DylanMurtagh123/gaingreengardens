“We’re not a faceless contractor — we’re a small family team that turns up, does the work properly, and treats every garden like it’s our own.”

Gain Green Gardens provides family-run gardening services across Ipswich and nearby Suffolk towns and villages. From neat weekly lawn mowing to hedge cutting, pruning, turf laying, garden tidy ups and overgrown clearances, the aim is simple: reliable gardening with a clean, cared-for finish.

Omari started the business around the values customers care about most — clear communication, fair quotes, tidy work and turning up when promised. Whether it is a regular maintenance visit or a one-off garden reset, every job is approached with the same attention to detail.

No hard sell, no surprise costs and no mess left behind — just friendly local help for gardens that need proper care.

“Every garden deserves patience, pride and a tidy finish. That is what we bring to every visit.”
