— Safety & suitability

The right help for
the right tree.

Not every tree cutting job is the same. Some garden trees are small and straightforward, while others need specialist equipment, climbing work, traffic management or professional tree surgery expertise.

Before quoting, we’ll look at the size of the tree, how easy it is to access, whether branches are near buildings or cables, and whether there may be any protection rules to check. This helps keep the work safe, suitable and properly planned.

Good to know:

  • Large or dangerous trees may need a tree surgeon
  • Protected trees may need council permission
  • Access and waste volume are checked before quoting
  • Minor tree work can often be paired with garden clearance
  • We’ll be clear if a job needs specialist support

— Tree cutting FAQs

Questions about
minor tree work?

Here are a few common questions about small tree cutting, branch removal and tree tidy ups in Ipswich.

Do you offer tree cutting in Ipswich?

Yes, minor tree cutting, small tree removal and branch removal can be arranged for homes in Ipswich and surrounding Suffolk areas.

Can you remove small trees from a garden?

Yes, small tree removal may be possible when the tree size, access and surrounding area make the work suitable. Larger or risky trees may need a qualified tree surgeon.

Do you remove branches and cuttings?

Yes, branch removal and green waste clearance can be included where agreed, helping leave the garden tidy after the tree work is complete.

Can you cut branches away from paths or lawns?

Yes, minor branch cutting can help clear overhanging growth from paths, lawns, borders and everyday garden areas.

Do I need permission before cutting a tree?

Some trees may be protected by a Tree Preservation Order or conservation area rules. If there is any doubt, it is best to check with the local council before arranging tree cutting.
